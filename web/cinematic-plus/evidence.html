Interpretation boundary

Strong evidence becomes weaker when its limits are hidden.

What this proves

  • The deterministic evaluation plumbing works.
  • Source self-report and independent evidence remain separate.
  • Bounded recovery paths are exercised and recorded.
  • Public replay and measurement paths are reproducible.

What it does not prove

  • Real hosted or local model performance.
  • Representative live reliability, provider cost or latency.
  • This is not production readiness or unrestricted-tool safety.
  • Universal model superiority or absence of every vulnerability.
