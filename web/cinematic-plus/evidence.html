Agent Completion Verifier

A model-agnostic evaluator that separates an agent's completion claim from independently observable evidence, then resolves the result against a fixed acceptance contract.

Luca: problem framing, acceptance standard, evidence interpretation, public boundary and final judgement.

ACE: research expansion, implementation candidates, testing support, alternatives and critique.

Problem

Fluent completion can hide an incomplete real state.

A successful tool receipt may prove that one action occurred without proving the requested outcome. Required fields may be absent, partial evidence may be promoted to completion, or a later rollback may invalidate an earlier success.

Completion is not a sentence generated by the system. It is a judgement against observable state.
