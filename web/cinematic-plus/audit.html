AI Agent Reliability Audit

For teams using AI agents or tool-calling workflows that need stronger evidence before an action is treated as complete.

Request a fit review View deliverables

ScopeOne clearly bounded workflow per initial engagement.

EvidenceRepresentative or redacted traces are preferred.

AuthorityNo production mutation or provider spend without separate written approval.

The problem

A convincing success message is not proof.

An agent may say an email was sent, a file was written or a record was updated while the required state is missing, partial or later rolled back.

  1. 01Define doneFix the observable completion contract first.
  2. 02Inspect tracesSeparate claims, actions, retries and side effects.
  3. 03Test failureExercise realistic false-success and partial-completion cases.
  4. 04Check stateVerify independent postconditions.
  5. 05Report findingsPrioritise repairs by severity and confidence.
  6. 06Plan remediationScope implementation separately.

What is delivered

One bounded workflow, examined end to end.

The audit produces concrete evidence and a repair path rather than a generic maturity score.

Suitable work

Best for consequential tool-using workflows.

Good fit

  • Email or messaging automation.
  • CRM, ticketing or database updates.
  • Document and file-generation pipelines.
  • Research or data-collection agents.
  • Multi-step operational workflows.
  • General versus specialist-agent orchestration.

Not included by default

  • Penetration testing.
  • Legal or compliance certification.
  • Formal safety certification.
  • Unrestricted production access.
  • Open-ended provider spending.
  • A guarantee that every hidden defect is eliminated.

Fit review

Send five facts, not sensitive data.

Describe the workflow's purpose, the systems it changes, what currently counts as done, one known failure or uncertainty, and whether representative or redacted evidence is available.

Do not send passwords, API keys, private customer data or unrestricted production credentials during initial contact.
