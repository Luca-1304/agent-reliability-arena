## Objective

Review one bounded AI-agent or tool-calling workflow to determine whether completion claims are supported by independently observable evidence and whether realistic failure, retry and rollback paths are handled safely.

## Included work

- map the workflow, tools, permissions and human approvals;
- define the observable completion contract;
- review the agreed evidence set;
- exercise the agreed deterministic or representative scenarios;
- identify confirmed false-success, partial-completion and evidence gaps;
- provide a prioritised findings report and implementation plan.

## Explicit exclusions

- penetration testing;
- legal or regulatory compliance certification;
- formal safety certification;
- unrestricted production access;
- production mutation unless separately authorised in writing;
- open-ended provider/tool spend;
- review of systems or workflows not named above;
- guarantee that all undiscovered defects are eliminated.

## Stop conditions

Work pauses when:

- requested access exceeds the agreed boundary;
- credentials or prohibited data appear unexpectedly;
- evidence integrity cannot be established;
- provider/tool spend would exceed the approved ceiling;
- production mutation is requested without separate written approval;
- the workflow is materially different from the scoped system.

## Acceptance

Delivery is complete when the agreed reports are supplied, the client has one review opportunity, factual corrections are resolved or recorded, and any implementation work is explicitly separated from the audit.
