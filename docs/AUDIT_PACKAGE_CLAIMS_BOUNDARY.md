# Audit delivery pack claims boundary

The template pack demonstrates a defined process for scoping, reviewing and reporting on one bounded AI-agent workflow. It does not demonstrate that an external client has purchased the service, that a client workflow has improved, that every listed control is appropriate for every domain, or that the service constitutes regulated assurance, legal advice, compliance certification or penetration testing.

Any future case study must distinguish:

- what the client supplied;
- what was independently observed;
- what was changed;
- what was retested;
- what remains uncertain;
- what the client explicitly approved for publication.
